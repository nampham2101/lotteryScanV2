## Manual steps

Screenshots go in `docs/evidence/4/`. To take one from the emulator, from the repo root:

```sh
adb exec-out screencap -p > docs/evidence/4/<name>.png
```

### Step 1: the app opens to the home screen

1. Start the `Pixel_10` emulator and install the app: from `app/`, run `fvm flutter run -d emulator-5554`, then stop it with `q`.
2. On the emulator, close the app if it is open, then open the app list and tap **Dò Vé Số**.

**Should happen:** the home screen appears, with the title, the line under it and the round button. It is not a blank white screen.

**Screenshot:** `home-screen.png`, the home screen right after opening.

### Step 2: the layout matches sketch 1

1. Put `home-screen.png` next to sketch 1 in `docs/design/screens.png` (the first phone, "1. Home: one button").
2. Compare them.

**Should happen:**

- From top to bottom: the title "Dò vé số" in large bold text, the line "Chụp vé để biết trúng hay không" in smaller text under it, then the button.
- The button is large and round (a circle, not a rounded rectangle), and filled blue like the sketch.
- Inside the button: a camera icon, with "Quét vé" in white under it.
- Everything is centred across the screen, on a white background.
- Nothing else is on screen: no top bar, no other buttons, icons or text.

**Screenshot:** `home-vs-sketch.png`, `home-screen.png` and sketch 1 side by side (any image tool is fine).

### Step 3: accents show correctly and nothing is cut off

1. On the open home screen, look closely at each text (zoom into `home-screen.png` if needed).

**Should happen:**

- "Dò vé số": the marks on ò, é and ố are all there; ố shows both its hat and its accent.
- "Chụp vé để biết trúng hay không": the dot under ụ, and the marks on é, ể, ế, ú and ô are all there.
- "Quét vé": the marks on both é are there.
- No letter shows as an empty box or a question mark.
- No text is cut at an edge, at the top of a tall accent, or by the circle of the button, and nothing ends in "…".

**Screenshot:** `accents.png`, a close-up crop of `home-screen.png` showing the three texts.

### Step 4: tapping the button does nothing

1. On the home screen, tap **Quét vé** once and wait two seconds.
2. Tap **Quét vé** five times quickly.
3. Wait two seconds.

**Should happen:** after each tap the screen stays exactly the same home screen. No new screen, no message, no popup, no error. The app does not close and does not show "Dò Vé Số keeps stopping".

**Screenshot:** `after-taps.png`, the screen after the five quick taps.
