# Tests for ticket #31: show the answer on the sketched result screens

Written from the ticket's "Done when" only, before the code exists.

**Where these run:** everything runs on the `Pixel_10` emulator, the automated tests and the manual steps. The ticket names no device, and the emulator can prove every item: the tests choose what ML Kit reads and what the results site answers, so no ticket has to be in front of the camera and the live feed isn't needed; airplane mode on the emulator cuts its internet for real; and the camera refusing or failing after "Quét vé khác" is made through the camera add-on, as in ticket #24, which works the same on any device. The manual steps look at screenshots the automated tests save on the same emulator. No item needs the Pixel 7.

## How the integration tests prove it

The tests look at the app the way a person does, so they accept any way of building the screens.

- **ML Kit's text and the site's answer come from the tests**, as in tickets #24 and #28. The camera, the kept photo, the screens and the touches are real. Unless a test says otherwise, the site answers with the saved feed of 11 November 2026, whose draw days run from 06/11 to 10/11/2026.
- **"A screen shows"** means: from its title down, its words, top to bottom, are exactly its own, and its button is the only one on screen. Texts side by side on one line count as one line, and a line break may stand in for a space, so the card may wrap as it does in the sketch. Something that can't be tapped above the title, such as an icon given a name for screen readers, isn't counted. 4a's words are "Chúc mừng!", then "Bạn trúng <giải>" and its amount for each prize, then the card, then "Quét vé khác". 4b's and 4c's are the title, the line below it, the card and "Quét vé khác". "Too old" and "couldn't load" have no card. A total would be a word too many, so "no total" is checked too.
- **"Đang đọc vé…"**: the ticket writes it with one "…" character, and the app wrote three dots before this ticket. A person can't tell them apart, so either passes.
- **The ticket card** is "Vé <số> · <tỉnh> · <DD/MM/YYYY>", with the province as the reading rule shows it, for example "TP. Hồ Chí Minh", and the date with slashes.
- **No photo:** after the shutter, the kept photo is searched for on a screenshot of the whole screen, at every size from a quarter of the screen's width up, above, below or behind the words. It must not be found on sketch 3 or on any answer screen.
- **Sketch 3 stays through the read and the feed load:** the first reading test keeps ML Kit's read unfinished and checks sketch 3 opens with no home screen in between, has no photo, and stays. The second holds the site's answer after the read has finished, and every look for 3 seconds must be sketch 3 and nothing else; it stops sooner if the request has waited 8 seconds, so the app's own 10 seconds never run out. Then the site answers, and every look until 4b shows must be sketch 3, nothing for a moment while the screen changes, or 4b on its way in; a `Số vé / Tỉnh / Ngày` line, another answer or 4d fails the test. The no-photo check isn't repeated while the feed loads, because finding a photo takes a few seconds that the held request doesn't have.
- **"Large type"**: the amount's text is at least 1.25 times as tall as its "Bạn trúng" text. If the app draws the two as one text, their sizes can't be told apart this way, and manual steps 2 and 3 show it.
- **"Nothing moves"**: once the screen shows, the test waits 0.7 seconds for the page change to settle, then takes screenshots for 3 seconds; each must match the first, apart from the status bar and the bottom bar. A movement that ends within the page change isn't seen.
- **"Highest first"** means the largest amount first. The tests use prizes where that's also the order of the prize table: Giải Sáu before Giải Tám, and Giải Nhất down to Giải Tám. Where may mắn and khuyến khích would sit among the others isn't tested.
- **Several prizes and scrolling:** 110433 against Hồ Chí Minh 09/11/2026 in the saved feed wins Giải Sáu (`0433`) and Giải Tám (`43`), and nothing else. For scrolling, the site answers with the saved feed with only Bạc Liêu's numbers on 10/11/2026 changed, so that 123456 wins one of each prize from Giải Nhất to Giải Tám, eight blocks, while Đặc biệt stays 267832, so no may mắn or khuyến khích. It isn't a likely draw, but it's the most prizes a ticket can win without them. The test swipes up slowly from the top until the screen stops moving: the lines that come into view, in order, must be exactly 4a's words, and "Quét vé khác" must come whole into view. On a screen where everything fits, nothing moves and everything is already in view, which also passes, since the criterion only asks for scrolling when the blocks don't fit. On `Pixel_10` eight blocks don't fit.
- **4c "today"** is the emulator's own date, read with `adb`. The province is one drawn on that weekday (for example Bến Tre on a Tuesday), though the app doesn't need it to be. "A later day" is two days after today. Both are after the saved feed's newest day, 10/11/2026, which is what makes them "not drawn yet". A run that starts just before midnight can see the date change in the middle of the test.
- **"Too old"** uses An Giang 05-11-2026, the day before the saved feed's oldest day.
- **"Couldn't load"** is brought about as in ticket #28: airplane mode for no internet (turned off again when the test ends, even if it fails), a site that never answers (the screen must not show before 9 seconds after the app asked, and must show by 12.5), and a site that answers at once with a maintenance page instead of the feed.
- **4d "unchanged from #24"** is ticket #24's own check: 4d's words, its one "Thử lại" button, and no photo above the words. Each test reaches it through sketch 3.
- **"The camera opens"** is ticket #24's check: the camera screen with its hint and its one shutter, Android saying the app is using the camera, and the same screen still there 3 seconds later.
- **"Thử lại" on "couldn't load"**: the site first answers with the maintenance page, so "couldn't load" shows for 111111 · Bạc Liêu · 10-11-2026. Then it holds requests, and "Thử lại" is tapped. Sketch 3 must open with no home screen in between and last; the app must ask for the feed again; sketch 3 must stay while that request is held. Then the site answers with the saved feed, and 4b for the same ticket must show, with only sketch 3 before it. "No new photo": ML Kit was asked to read once in the whole test, the photos inside the app are exactly the ones from before the tap, and Android says the app isn't using the camera.
- **"Home shows"** after back means the home screen, still there 3 seconds later.
- **Camera won't start after "Quét vé khác"** uses ticket #24's checks: the "Cần dùng camera" pop-up appears with its words and its "Đóng" button, and closing it leaves the home screen; or the note "Lỗi camera, thử lại" shows on the home screen, is seen for at least 2 seconds and is gone within 4.5. The criterion is the same on all four screens, so refusal is tested on 4b and the other failure on "too old".
- **No errors:** every test that ends on an answer, a button or back also checks the app raised no error since it began. The camera-won't-start tests don't, as in ticket #24, because a camera that won't start can make the camera add-on report one.

**Not tested:**

- 4c's second line for a ticket dated before today but after the feed's newest day (the criteria cover today and later days only).
- Back on sketch 3 itself, which no criterion of this ticket mentions. Ticket #18's tests still expect home.
- Where may mắn and khuyến khích sit in "highest first" (see above).

## Changes to older tests

Ticket #31 drops the temporary screen, so every older test that expected the photo on screen, `Đang đọc vé...` below it, the three `Số vé / Tỉnh / Ngày` lines or ticket #28's answer lines below them changes on purpose. The shared helpers changed as listed under "Files" above. Nothing else in these tests changed.

- **What a scan shows:** after the shutter, the tests now wait for sketch 3 (`givenReadingShows`, `givenPhotoTaken`, `expectReadingScreenOpensWithoutHome`), and after the read for the full answer screen.
- **`fullTicketText`**, the read that works for tests not about the reading, is now Bến Tre 10-11-2026, 123456, which loses on a day in the saved feed, so it shows 4b with its card. It was Bến Tre 05-11-2026, "too old", which has no card. It moved from `helpers/ticket_fields.dart` to `helpers/answer_screens.dart`, next to its answer.
- **Telling two photos apart:** with no photo on screen, the tests that take a new photo after an earlier one tell them apart by their answers. The earlier ticket (Đà Lạt 24-11-2019) stays "too old"; the new one (Bạc Liêu 111222) is now dated 10-11-2026 instead of 20-10-2026, so it shows 4b with its own card.
- **Ticket #24's field tests** read the fields from the ticket card instead of the three lines. Their file now has the site answer with the saved feed moved back to 2015 (2015 has the same calendar as 2026). Every ticket there is dated after 10/11/2015, so by ticket #28's rule it isn't drawn yet, and 4c shows its card, whatever its province. The card writes the date with slashes, so `Ngày: 30-10-2026` becomes the card's `30/10/2026`.

## Running the automated tests

Start the emulator with `fvm flutter emulators --launch Pixel_10`. From `app/`:

```sh
export ANDROID_SERIAL=emulator-5554
fvm flutter drive -d "$ANDROID_SERIAL" \
  --driver=integration_test/answer_screens_driver.dart \
  --target=integration_test/camera_start_app.dart
```

The emulator doesn't need the internet: the "no internet" test cuts it on purpose, and every other test gets a feed from the tests. Don't touch the emulator during a run. If a run stops in the middle of the "no internet" test, check that airplane mode is off afterwards: `adb -s "$ANDROID_SERIAL" shell cmd connectivity airplane-mode disable`. If every test stops in SETUP with the home screen not showing, the emulator is the problem, not the app (backlog item 55): `adb -s emulator-5554 emu kill`, then `fvm flutter emulators --launch Pixel_10 --cold`, and wait a couple of minutes.

The updated older tests, and only those. `flutter drive` hands `--name` to `dart test`, which runs the tests whose full names (group and test) match. Keep the patterns free of commas, because `flutter drive` splits its arguments at commas.

```sh
fvm flutter drive -d "$ANDROID_SERIAL" \
  --driver=integration_test/photo_screen_driver.dart \
  --target=integration_test/ticket_text_app.dart

fvm flutter drive -d "$ANDROID_SERIAL" \
  --driver=integration_test/ticket_text_driver.dart \
  --target=integration_test/ticket_text_app.dart

fvm flutter drive -d "$ANDROID_SERIAL" \
  --driver=integration_test/photo_crop_driver.dart \
  --target=integration_test/photo_crop_app.dart

fvm flutter drive -d "$ANDROID_SERIAL" \
  --driver=integration_test/ticket_fields_driver.dart \
  --target=integration_test/ticket_text_app.dart

fvm flutter drive -d "$ANDROID_SERIAL" \
  --driver=integration_test/camera_start_driver.dart \
  --target=integration_test/camera_start_app.dart \
  --test-arguments=test --test-arguments=--reporter=expanded \
  '--test-arguments=--name=Given 4d shows'

fvm flutter drive -d "$ANDROID_SERIAL" \
  --driver=integration_test/results_driver.dart \
  --target=integration_test/ticket_text_app.dart \
  --test-arguments=test --test-arguments=--reporter=expanded \
  '--test-arguments=--name=^Won |^Lost |^Outside'
```

These tests are not picked up by `fvm flutter test integration_test`, because they run on the Mac.
