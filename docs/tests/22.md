# Tests for ticket #22: crop the taken photo to the dashed frame

Written from the ticket's "Done when" only, before the code exists.

**Where these run:** all the automated tests run on the `Pixel_10` emulator, because the ticket names no device for them and the emulator can prove them. Its back camera sees a still room, so a screenshot of the dashed frame just before the shutter shows exactly what the photo should show, and `adb emu rotate` gives a second view with nobody holding anything. The tests choose what ML Kit answers and can spoil the camera's picture, so they need neither a ticket nor a real crop failure. Manual step 1 runs on the Pixel 7, because the ticket names that phone for it and only a real camera can look at a real ticket.

## How the integration tests prove it

The tests look at the app the way a person does, so they accept any way of cropping: any add-on, any file name, any folder.

- **"Exactly what was inside the dashed frame"** takes a screenshot of the camera screen just before the shutter. The frame is found in it the way a person sees it: the one big square of camera picture on the dark screen. The kept photo is then stretched over that square and compared with it, square by square, leaving out the dashed edge and the rounded corners. A photo of more, of less, of another part or of another shape doesn't match. Measured on the emulator's room against photos from the app before this ticket: the middle square of the camera's photo scores about 5 and 7 in the camera's two views, and a square cut just 3% smaller about 12 and 17. The limit is 10. The photo comes out a little brighter than the live picture all over, so an even difference in brightness doesn't count.
- **"A square photo"** reads the kept file, turned the way its own note says. Its sides may differ by 1%, for rounding.
- **"Straight away", "shown whole" and "below it"** are checked as in ticket #18: the read is kept unfinished, so the screen can only open before ML Kit answers, and the kept photo must be found on screen whole and upright, above the text.
- **"The text it read from the cropped photo"**: the picture ML Kit was given must be the kept file, by a checksum of its contents, and that file is the one checked square and matching the frame. The text on screen must then be ML Kit's answer letter by letter, as in ticket #18.
- **"Only one photo, the cropped one"** looks for photos by content, whatever their name: everywhere in the app's private folders, cache included, and in its folders on the shared storage. There must be exactly one, and it must be square and match the frame. The gallery must be the same as before. The test also checks the very file the camera saved its picture to: it must be gone, unless the app cropped that file in place and kept it as its one photo. The test starts with an earlier photo kept, so a photo left from before counts too.
- **Making the crop fail.** `photo_crop_app.dart` reaches the camera's finished picture before the app gets it, and replaces its contents with the start of a JPEG and nothing a decoder can use. Any crop fails on it, the way a real crop fails on a picture it can't use. A leftover copy still counts as a photo when the folders are searched. The one limit: the app must take its photo with the camera add-on's `takePicture`, as it does today. If no picture comes through there, the test stops with a SETUP message.
- **"The camera screen stays open"** is watched many times a second, from the shutter tap until three seconds after the note has gone. Every look must show the camera screen, never home or the photo screen. Then Android must still say the app is using the camera.
- **"Near the bottom"** means the whole note is in the bottom third of the screen.
- **"Fades after a few seconds"** means the note goes away by itself: it stays for at least 1.5 seconds, measured from the first look that saw it, so a note shown for two seconds passes, and it's gone within 10 seconds. How the fade looks isn't checked.
- **The note's words** must be exactly "Lỗi xử lí ảnh. Vui lòng chụp lại ảnh khác", with every accent. A line break in place of a space is allowed.
- **"No photo is kept from this try, and any photo kept before stays unchanged"** lists every photo inside the app, with its time, size and a checksum of its contents, before the shutter and after the note has gone. They must be the same, nothing new may be in the app's shared-storage folders or the gallery, and the camera's file from the failed try must be gone.
- **"A new photo is taken" after the note**: the second tap comes while the note is showing. The camera must have taken a second picture, and the photo screen must open with that photo, square and matching the frame, and its own read.
- **"Press back before the temporary screen opens"**: the camera's picture is held back until the back button has been pressed, so the screen can't open first. It's then let through, so it arrives while the person is leaving. The home screen must show and stay, no photo screen may appear at any look, the photos must be the same as before, and the camera's file must be gone. The tests can't hold the crop itself, only the picture before it. This test may already pass before the feature exists, because the app already drops a photo that arrives after the person has left. It stays as a guard, because the crop adds a step between the picture and the screen.
- **"Only the new cropped photo and its own text"** takes a photo, reads it, goes home, turns the emulator's camera to the other view and takes a second one. The second must be square and match the frame of the second view, the screen must show it and not the first, its own text only must show, and the app must hold only that photo.

## Changes to the tests of tickets #10 and #18

None were needed. The ticket says their tests that check the shown photo will change on purpose, but those tests compare the screen with the photo the app keeps, whatever its shape, and find it at any size. Once the app keeps the cropped photo, they compare the screen with the cropped photo, which is what the temporary screen will show. Whether that photo is the right crop is checked here. No shared helper was changed, so older tests weren't run.

`docs/tests/10.md` and `docs/tests/18.md` weren't changed either. Their manual steps still hold: #10's step 1 expects at least everything inside the dashed frame, as wide as the screen, and #18's step 1 expects the photo of the ticket whole and not cut off.

## Running the automated tests

Start the emulator with `fvm flutter emulators --launch Pixel_10`. From `app/`:

```sh
export ANDROID_SERIAL=emulator-5554
fvm flutter drive -d "$ANDROID_SERIAL" \
  --driver=integration_test/photo_crop_driver.dart \
  --target=integration_test/photo_crop_app.dart
```

Don't touch the emulator during a run. If a run is stopped by force in the middle of the last test, the emulator's camera stays turned. To put it back, run `adb -s emulator-5554 emu rotate` until the camera screen shows the room upright, with the TV standing on its cabinet.

If every test stops in SETUP with the home screen not showing, the emulator is the problem, not the app. A stuck notification shade and Android's "isn't responding" pop-up both did this before (backlog item 55). What cleared it: `adb -s emulator-5554 emu kill`, then `fvm flutter emulators --launch Pixel_10 --cold`, then waiting a couple of minutes before starting the tests.

These tests are not picked up by `fvm flutter test integration_test`, because only the Mac can take the screenshots, read the kept photo and turn the emulator's camera.

## Manual steps

Screenshots go in `docs/evidence/22/`. They go in this public repository: before saving one, check that it shows only the ticket, the object you put next to it and the table, with no people, faces, hands or feet.

### Before you start

1. Connect the Pixel 7 and check that `adb devices` lists it as `device`.
2. Put its serial in `ANDROID_SERIAL`: `export ANDROID_SERIAL=<serial from adb devices>`. Don't write it in any file.
3. Install the app: from `app/`, run `fvm flutter run -d "$ANDROID_SERIAL"`, then stop it with `q`.
4. Have a real lottery ticket you're happy to show, on a plain flat surface with good light, and a small object that is easy to recognise, such as a pen or a coin.

### Step 1: a real ticket, with something just outside the frame

1. Open **Dò Vé Số** on the Pixel 7.
2. Tap **Quét vé**. If Android asks, tap **While using the app**.
3. Hold the phone over the ticket so the ticket fills the dashed frame and its print looks sharp.
4. Slide the object towards the ticket from above, until its tip just appears at the top edge of the frame. Then slide it back out about 2 cm, so it's just outside the frame and can't be seen on the camera screen. Keep the phone still from here on.
5. From the repo root, take the screenshot: `adb -s "$ANDROID_SERIAL" exec-out screencap -p > docs/evidence/22/real-ticket-camera-pixel7.png`.
6. Tap the shutter, and keep watching the screen.
7. Wait until "Đang đọc vé..." goes away.
8. Take the screenshot: `adb -s "$ANDROID_SERIAL" exec-out screencap -p > docs/evidence/22/real-ticket-cropped-pixel7.png`.

**Should happen:**

- In `real-ticket-camera-pixel7.png`, the ticket fills the frame and the object can't be seen.
- In `real-ticket-cropped-pixel7.png`, the photo on top shows the ticket the way the frame showed it in `real-ticket-camera-pixel7.png`: the same part of the ticket, square, whole and not cut off. The object does not show anywhere in the photo, not even its tip.
- Words from the ticket show below the photo, such as "XỔ SỐ KIẾN THIẾT". ML Kit may misread some of them: this step proves the ticket's words reach the screen, not that every word is right.
- "Không đọc được vé" must not show. If it does, try again with better light before calling it a failure, and note the try in the evidence.

**Screenshots:** `real-ticket-camera-pixel7.png`, `real-ticket-cropped-pixel7.png`.
