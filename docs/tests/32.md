## How the widget tests prove it

Each screen is built the way the app builds it (`answerScreenFor`, `CouldNotLoadScreen`, `CantReadScreen`) and opened as a new page on top of another screen, on a screen the Pixel_10's size. The test's own clock moves one frame (16 ms) at a time, so every timing is exact. The tests don't depend on how the movement is built: they measure how large each thing is drawn on screen, and compare pictures of the whole screen.

- **Time zero** is the first frame the screen is drawn. The page change is drawn without movement of its own, so anything that moves is the screen's own.
- **"Pop in, growing from small":** the icon, "Chúc mừng!" and each amount are measured at every frame. A thing counts as shown once it's there, has a size, and isn't faded out (below 5% see-through). When first shown, it's at most 60% of its final width.
- **"With a slight bounce":** once it has reached its size (within 2%), it goes past it or falls back from it by at least 2% before settling. "Slight" means it's never drawn larger than 1.5 times its size, nor smaller than 60% of it once it got there. This accepts both an overshoot and a bounce back, since the ticket doesn't say which.
- **"One after another":** each is first shown at least 50 ms after the one before it, in the order icon, "Chúc mừng!", then each amount from the top. Whether the "Bạn trúng" lines, the card or the button move isn't tested: the ticket doesn't say.
- **Confetti** is what changes outside the screen's own things: every text, the icon, the ticket card and the button, each with room around it for a slight bounce. Fewer than 4 changed sample points don't count.
  - **"As they pop in":** it's first seen no later than 0.5 s after the last thing started to pop in.
  - **"Bursts from the icon":** where it's first seen is centred within 160 px of the icon's centre (the icon is 56 px).
  - **"Falls down the screen":** it reaches the bottom quarter of the screen.
  - **"For about 2 seconds":** from first seen to last seen takes 1.3 to 3 seconds, the same allowance as ticket #24's "about 3 seconds" (2 to 4.5).
  - **"Once":** it's never gone for more than 0.5 s before it's over.
  - **"Then the screen stays still":** nothing on screen changes from 0.5 s after the last confetti until 6 s, and Flutter has nothing left to draw.
- **"Doesn't play again":** after 6 s, the screen is pictured every 0.25 s for 60 s and must not change.
- **4b, "about half a second later":** nothing changes for the first 0.25 s, and the first change comes by 1 s.
- **4b, "the smiley grows slightly":** the smiley is what's drawn above "Chưa trúng lần này". At its largest it's 1.05 to 1.5 times as wide as when still. Nothing at or below the title moves.
- **4b, "then the screen stays still":** the movement is over by 3 s, after which the screen is exactly as it was when it opened, for 60 s more.
- **Nothing moves** on 4c, "too old", "couldn't load" and 4d: from time zero, pictured every 48 ms for 3 s and every 0.25 s up to 10 s, the screen never changes, and Flutter has nothing left to draw.

## How the integration tests prove it

They run the real app as ticket #31's tests do. A screenshot of the emulator takes about 2 seconds, too slow to watch a movement of about 2 seconds, so the app says itself whether anything on screen is moving: whether an animation will draw another frame (`app/integration_test/movement_report.dart`). That's what Flutter Driver itself waits on before it taps.

- **"It moves":** the page change to 4a or 4b takes 450 ms on Android. Between 0.5 and 1.5 s after the screen first shows, the app must be seen moving at least once.
- **"Exactly as in #31":** once nothing has moved for a second, a screenshot is compared with the one ticket #31 saved for the same ticket: `docs/evidence/31/4a-one-prize.png`, `4a-two-prizes.png` and `4b.png`. The comparison is #31's own "nothing moves" one: sampled pixels, without the status bar and the bottom bar, at most 2 differing. It's checked again after 10 seconds, during which the app must never be moving ("doesn't play again").
- **Pressing while it moves:** each test first opens the screen, waits until its movement has ended, presses, and times how long the camera or home takes to show. Then it scans the same ticket again and presses as soon as the app is seen moving, from 0.5 s after the screen showed. The camera must open, or home show, at most 1 s (camera) or 0.3 s (back) later than on the still screen, and stay. Waiting for 4a's movement to end would take over a second more.
  - The tap and the back press are made inside the app, so they land at once: Flutter Driver taps "Quét vé khác" through Flutter's own touch handling, with its wait for animations turned off, and back is the same call the phone's back button reaches (`handlePopRoute`). A back press or tap through `adb` takes long enough for 4b's short movement to be over. The real back button and real taps on still screens are already ticket #31's tests.
- **No errors:** each test checks the app raised no error since its last scan began. Going home from the camera between the two scans can raise the camera add-on's known "release a second time" error (see `camera_start_app.dart`), which comes before that and isn't counted.

**Not tested, or only partly:**

- The wink itself, and what the confetti looks like: manual steps 1 to 3.
- "Straight away" on 4b: a wait for 4b's movement to end would add under a second, which the 1-second camera allowance can't tell apart. The 0.3-second back allowance can.
- Whether anything moves on 4a with eight prizes (ticket #31's scrolling test). The criteria use one and several prizes, and the tests use two.
- The movement on a real phone.

## Running the automated tests

Widget tests, from `app/`, with no device:

```sh
fvm flutter test test/answer_movement_test.dart
```

Integration tests: start the emulator with `fvm flutter emulators --launch Pixel_10`. From `app/`:

```sh
export ANDROID_SERIAL=emulator-5554
fvm flutter drive -d "$ANDROID_SERIAL" \
  --driver=integration_test/answer_movement_driver.dart \
  --target=integration_test/camera_start_app.dart
```

The updated older tests, and only those (keep the pattern free of commas, because `flutter drive` splits its arguments at commas):

```sh
fvm flutter drive -d "$ANDROID_SERIAL" \
  --driver=integration_test/answer_screens_driver.dart \
  --target=integration_test/camera_start_app.dart \
  --test-arguments=test --test-arguments=--reporter=expanded \
  '--test-arguments=--name=^Answers Given a ticket that wins (one prize|several prizes|nothing)'
```

Don't touch the emulator during a run. If every test stops in SETUP with the home screen not showing, the emulator is the problem, not the app (backlog item 55): `adb -s emulator-5554 emu kill`, then `fvm flutter emulators --launch Pixel_10 --cold`, and wait a couple of minutes. "Exactly as in #31" fails with a SETUP message if the emulator's screen isn't 1080 x 2340, the size #31's screenshots were taken at.

## Manual steps

Recordings and screenshots go in `docs/evidence/32/`. The integration tests record the screen, so nothing is run twice: each step looks at one recording.

### Before you start

1. Start the emulator: `fvm flutter emulators --launch Pixel_10`, and wait until its home screen shows.
2. Record the movement: from `app/`, run the tests that record it. `MOVEMENT_RECORDINGS` names the folder; without it, the tests record nothing.

```sh
export ANDROID_SERIAL=emulator-5554
MOVEMENT_RECORDINGS=../docs/evidence/32 fvm flutter drive -d "$ANDROID_SERIAL" \
  --driver=integration_test/answer_movement_driver.dart \
  --target=integration_test/camera_start_app.dart \
  --test-arguments=test --test-arguments=--reporter=expanded \
  '--test-arguments=--name=^Once the movement has ended'
```

3. Check that `docs/evidence/32/` now holds `4a-one-prize.mp4`, `4a-two-prizes.mp4` and `4b.mp4`. Each starts on the camera screen, then the shutter, sketch 3, and the answer screen until its movement has ended.

Watch each recording at normal speed first, then step through it frame by frame (in QuickTime: pause, then the left and right arrow keys). To take a screenshot of a frame, pause on it and use Cmd+Shift+4 on the video, then save it under the name given.

### Step 1: 4a with one prize

Open `4a-one-prize.mp4`.

**Should happen:** when 4a appears, the green party-popper icon pops in first, growing from small, going a little past its size and settling back. Then "Chúc mừng!", then "100.000đ", each the same way, one after another. As they pop in, small coloured pieces of confetti burst out from the icon and fall down the screen for about 2 seconds, then they're gone. Nothing else moves. After that, 4a looks exactly like `docs/evidence/31/4a-one-prize.png` and stays still.

**Screenshots:** `4a-confetti-burst.png`, a frame where the confetti is spreading out from the icon while "100.000đ" is still popping in; `4a-confetti-falling.png`, a frame where the confetti is falling in the lower half of the screen.

### Step 2: 4a with two prizes

Open `4a-two-prizes.mp4`.

**Should happen:** as step 1, with the icon, then "Chúc mừng!", then "400.000đ", then "100.000đ" popping in one after another, each with the same slight bounce. The confetti bursts once, not once per amount, and falls for about 2 seconds. After that, 4a looks exactly like `docs/evidence/31/4a-two-prizes.png` and stays still.

**Screenshot:** `4a-two-prizes-popping.png`, a frame where "400.000đ" has popped in and "100.000đ" is still small or not yet shown.

### Step 3: 4b

Open `4b.mp4`.

**Should happen:** 4b appears still. About half a second later, the grey smiley grows a little, closes one eye and opens it again once (a wink), and goes back to its size. Nothing else moves. After that, 4b looks exactly like `docs/evidence/31/4b.png` and stays still.

**Screenshot:** `4b-wink.png`, the frame where the smiley is grown with one eye closed.
