Permissions in the release APK built after fix round 1 (aapt2 dump permissions on app-release.apk).
CAMERA is the only dangerous permission. ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE is a normal permission, and the
DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION one is the app's own, from AndroidX. RECORD_AUDIO, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE and
READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE came from the camera add-on (camera_android_camerax; the merger added READ because WRITE was
requested) and are removed with tools:node="remove" in AndroidManifest.xml.

package: com.nampham.lotteryscan
uses-permission: name='android.permission.CAMERA'
permission: com.nampham.lotteryscan.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION
uses-permission: name='com.nampham.lotteryscan.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION'
uses-permission: name='android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE'
